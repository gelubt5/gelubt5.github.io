🕊️ Pigeon racing platforms

Evidența Columbofilă

My main project since 2017: a complete management platform for pigeon fanciers, with pigeon records, a pedigree generator (PDF/JPG export), competition results, breeding planning and health tracking. Laravel, SQLite, Vue.js.

evidentacolumbofila.eu

xPigeon app Android + web

The companion app for Evidența Columbofilă, built with Expo / React Native: native pedigrees, a pair generator with inbreeding coefficient, a breeding calendar, a gene-based color calculator, sale ads, auctions, a 3D museum and 3D shop for pigeons, and an AI assistant.

xpigeon.netGoogle Play

Columba – Race map & rankings

A Romanian pigeon racing competition platform: live race map, live rankings by zone and center, marathon and extreme reports, Super AS Maraton and national palmares reports. PHP, SQLite, Leaflet.

hartaconcurs.online

Distanța app Android

An Android app for pigeon fanciers: distance calculations, GPS capture, fuel cost calculators and weather/wind forecasts (Open-Meteo). Published on Google Play.

PigeonTag hardware

Hardware R&D for pigeon tracking: an nRF52832 BLE beacon, a GPS+LTE tracker based on nRF9161, and 3D-printable NFC leg rings, with PCBs designed in KiCad.

Pigeon fanciers social network planned

A social network connected to Evidența Columbofilă. Members are grouped into leagues and series and are promoted or relegated by activity: giveaways, raffles, auctions and interviews with top breeders.

Veterinary advisor & shopping bots

AI chatbots for bird keepers: a veterinary advisor built from veterinary product catalogs and the EU medicines database (doses with links to official sources), and a shopping assistant for buying pigeon products.
